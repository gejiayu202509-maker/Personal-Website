Maintainer: Ge Jiayu

Original website: https://gejiayu.top/

## Homepage artwork

`assets/hero-bg.webp` is an AI-assisted custom illustration created for Ge Jiayu's
personal homepage and based on his appearance. It is not a documentary photo or
a generic background supplied with a reusable website template.

Ge Jiayu does **not** grant permission to copy, republish, adapt, crop, or use this
artwork as another person's website imagery without his explicit permission.
Adding credit does not substitute for permission. The permission to adapt the
website's ideas, layout, styles, and code does not include this artwork.

When adapting the website, supply your own image and update social previews,
preload references, alt text, structured data, and image metadata accordingly.
Do not imply that Ge Jiayu's identity-based imagery depicts you.

## Other images

Personal portraits, photos, and biographical material are not included in the
code reuse permission. Third-party posters, covers, and other media are not
claimed as Ge Jiayu's creations and retain their own rights and terms. This
update adds a source signature only to the custom homepage illustration, not
to third-party media or the other personal photos.

## What the source markers do

The homepage artwork includes a small visible signature in its pixels and XMP
metadata identifying Ge Jiayu and the original website. The image fingerprint
and an earlier public asset fingerprint are recorded in `provenance.json`.

These are source indications, not access control, a digital signature, a C2PA
Content Credential, or a legal certificate of authorship. They can be removed or
changed. The earlier unmarked asset remains in the existing public Git history;
this update does not erase past downloads or rewrite that history.

For permission questions, use https://gejiayu.top/contact.html.
