我是谁 Who I Am

我叫葛家羽（Ge Jiayu），在海南海口长大，目前在香港大学计算与数据科学学院读大二，走 AI & Data Science 方向。
I grew up in Haikou, Hainan. Now a sophomore at HKU's School of Computing and Data Science, on the AI & Data Science track.

我想把 AI 和数据方法用在真实问题上：从投研证据整理，到雅思写作与口语训练产品，我喜欢把模糊的想法做成能用、能继续改进的系统。
I am drawn to AI and data work that becomes useful in the real world. From research evidence workflows to an IELTS writing and speaking tool, I like turning an unclear idea into a system people can use and improve.

我花了三年从理科实验班的底部爬出来，用全省前 0.3% 的成绩换了一张去香港的离场券——不是为了逃离，是为了走向更自由的可能性。我做过项目助理，操盘过三场大型赛事；在投资机构见过真实的商业世界。拿过专利，赢过竞赛，踢过冠军，甩掉过 18 公斤。没有一个标签能单独定义我，但合在一起，它们就是我的形状。
Three years to claw back from the bottom of an elite science class. Top 0.3% in the province bought me a ticket to Hong Kong — not to escape, but to walk toward something freer. I've helped run large-scale events, worked in investment research, was granted a utility model patent, won competitions, played on a league-winning football team, and lost more than 18 kg through consistent training. No single label defines me. Together, they trace the shape of who I'm becoming.

我不在一个框架里寻找身份。我在行动中认识自己。
I don't find identity in a box. I find it in motion.

我怎么做 How I Work

做事稳，响应快。习惯把复杂任务拆成可执行的步骤，保持节奏，注重交付。遇到不会的就学——这是我高中三年学到的最重要的事。
Steady hands, fast response. I break complexity into executable steps, keep the rhythm, and care about what gets delivered. When I don't know something, I learn it — that's the most important skill high school taught me.

做项目时，我会先找清楚问题，再把信息整理成可追溯的步骤，快速做出第一版并反复修正。编程和数据分析还在持续学习；项目执行、跨方沟通与 AI 辅助开发，已经在真实工作中用过。
I start by understanding the problem, organizing evidence into workable steps, and improving a first version through use. I am still developing my programming and data skills; project execution, cross-team communication, and AI-assisted building are things I have put to work.
